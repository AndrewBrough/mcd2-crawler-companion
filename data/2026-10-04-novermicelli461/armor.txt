ARMOR SETS MATTER MORE THAN BEFORE (NEW)
Every ARMOR set is tagged with one or more archetypes, and each archetype has its own pool of effects. The set decides which effects a piece can roll. (Credit to u/27Nrodoom who spotted this from rerolling Ranger leggings!)
