Combo = damage of each hit in the combo (x2 / x3 = that swing hits more than once).
Time = how long the whole combo takes if you keep attacking. Base DPS = total / time.
Reach = attack range (bigger = hits from further away). Sorted by Base DPS.

THERE ARE 8 DAMAGE TYPES (NEW)
Melee, ranged, fire, ice, lightning, poison, soul and blast. Damage number colours show the type, e.g. purple = soul. Some unique weapons deal elemental damage instead of normal melee damage, so they get boosted by the matching artifact effect (Pyromancer, Soulmancer, etc.):


- Soul: Soul Reaper (Scythe), Spectral Spear (Short Spear), Sculker's Bane (Twilight Dagger), Cacaphonous Cleaver (Meat Cleaver), Lullaby Blade (Cookiecutter)
- Fire: The Burning Blade (Sword)
- Ice: Frostbiter (Longsword), Prime Enchanter's Gauntlets (Gauntlets) (one hand)
- Lightning: Stormcaller (Pike), Prime Enchanter's Gauntlets (Gauntlets) (other hand)
- Poison: Venomous Fangs (Double Daggers)
- Blast: Heartbreaker (War Hammer)

MORE FINDINGS (NEW)
- The Blizzard Staff fires real projectiles, which is probably why ranged enchantments like Ricochet affect it (great find from u/LeastKaleidoscope359).
- Armour = Item Power (how tanky you are) + set (which effects it can roll) + effects.
